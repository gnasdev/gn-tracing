Inspector capture

Storage and DOM capture are on by default with full recording. They capture more sensitive data (storage, cookies, DOM text) and increase package size. Keep redaction on to mask values that match sensitive patterns. They lock on while network capture is enabled.

Capture mode

CDP is the default and captures with full fidelity, but shows a chrome.debugger banner. In-page mode avoids the banner but has lower fidelity (no cross-origin response bodies, no real source maps).
