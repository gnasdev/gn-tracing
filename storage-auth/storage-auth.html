GN Tracing

Connect cloud storage

Connect Google Drive or Dropbox here. The extension popup only lets you pick an already-connected provider for upload.

What you should know before connecting

GN Tracing helps you record a browser tab and save a replay package. Recording only starts when you press Start in the extension. Packages are uploaded to your own cloud account—not to a GN Tracing server. The app does not watch your browsing in the background and does not collect product analytics.

When you connect a cloud, what can the app do?

You choose Google Drive or Dropbox. The app only asks for limited access needed to save and share replays—not full control of your entire cloud account.

Google Drive access

  • Uses the drive.file scope only—files the app creates or that you open with the app.
  • Can upload replay packages, read those packages for playback, and create shareable links.
  • Cannot browse or edit your other Drive files, Gmail, Photos, or full account content.

Dropbox access

  • Can upload and read replay packages, create and read share links, and see basic account info (for connection status).
  • Does not request full Dropbox access to every folder outside what the app needs for those packages.

Where your login stays

  • Connection tokens stay in this browser’s extension storage on your device.
  • You can disconnect anytime on this page. That stops new uploads until you connect again.

Optional Feedback (topbar) is sent only when you submit it. It may create a public issue with your message and light diagnostics (app version, browser, OS, language)—never passwords or recording contents.
