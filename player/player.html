Browser debugging extension

GN Tracing

Purpose of GN Tracing

GN Tracing does not run continuous background surveillance. Recording starts only when you click record in the extension popup and stops when you stop recording or close the tab.

What GN Tracing does

  • Records tab video and optional tab audio
  • Captures console, network, and WebSocket debugging data
  • Applies client-side redaction based on your privacy settings
  • Generates a shareable replay link for the hosted player

How to use GN Tracing

Cloud storage access

  • Supports Google Drive and Dropbox (user-owned cloud only).
  • Packages stay in your account; SharePoint/site drives are not supported.
  • Replay files are link-readable so shared URLs work; optional zip passwords protect contents.
