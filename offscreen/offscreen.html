Choose what to share to start recording

Firefox only starts screen sharing from a click on this page, and it shares a window or a whole screen — it cannot share a single tab. Pick the Firefox window holding the page below; sharing an entire screen also records everything else on it. Keep this tab open until you stop the recording.

Console and network evidence needs access to the recorded site. Without it the video is still recorded, but console and network stay empty once the page navigates.
